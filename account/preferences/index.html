Language

Translation powered by Google Translate. Switching back to English reloads the page.

Text-to-speech

Uses your browser's built-in voices, which depend on your device. On any page, select text and press Alt + Shift + S to hear it read aloud (press again to stop).

This browser can't read text aloud.

Head tracking cursor

Uses your webcam and MediaPipe face tracking, in the same way as Open Coding Society's NodCursor. Video stays on your device; only your calibration numbers are saved.

Calibration

Turn tracking on, then look at each spot and press its button: center, then the far left, right, top and bottom of your screen.
